Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026

Task (com.500nits.Task) does not collect or send your personal data. This policy explains what stays on your Mac.

What Task Does Not Collect

  • Personal information, such as your name or email address.
  • Account, registration, or login details.
  • Usage data, analytics, or tracking.
  • Your Desktop names, colors, or configuration on any server.

Local Data

Task saves your Desktop names, their badge colors, and its settings in one plain-text file. The file stays on your Mac and is never sent to an external server.

Third-Party Services

Task does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or any other service that collects your data.

Children's Privacy

Task is not designed for children under 4 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may change from time to time. Any update will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
