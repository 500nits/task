Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 23, 2026

Task (com.500nits.Task) ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our app handles information.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No personal information (e.g., name, email address).

Local Data

Task stores your Desktop names, their badge colours, and its own settings in a single plain-text file on your Mac. This information remains on your device and is not sent to external servers.

We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or services that collect user data.

Our app is not targeted at children under 4. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 4.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
